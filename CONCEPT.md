# favorcraft — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A cross-faction Merchant's Favor profession and recipe planner evolved from Redridge Recipe Planner, combining recipe budgeting with materials and profession scanning.

## Historical direction and constraints

The README attributes the initial idea to **Danoozy**, with Babuni.dev building and refining the project. That co-authorship must remain visible. FavorcraftDB is intentionally retained for saved-plan compatibility with the earlier tool; /rrp and /redridge aliases remain.

Reconstructed from the current README, TOCs and source files; not a complete historical transcript.

## Project evolution and human contributions

Danoozy proposed the original planner idea. Babuni.dev built and refined it, expanding the Redridge-oriented recipe planning concept into a shared Alliance/Horde Merchant's Favor catalog with profession material requirements, filters and comparisons. The practical player problem is deciding what to buy, what the purchase costs and what its craft requires. Legacy aliases and saved data preserve continuity for existing users.

The current source excludes five profession certifications from the **309 recipe** list, explaining why some public vendor counts reach **314 offers**. Those numbers are documentation of the October source snapshot, not fresh verification of the live game.
