# favorcraft — concept

**Author:** Babuni.dev

A cross-faction Merchant's Favor profession and recipe planner evolved from Redridge Recipe Planner, combining recipe budgeting with materials and profession scanning.

## Historical direction and constraints

The README attributes the initial idea to **Danoozy**, with Babuni.dev building and refining the project. That co-authorship must remain visible. FavorcraftDB is intentionally retained for saved-plan compatibility with the earlier tool; /rrp and /redridge aliases remain.
